The letter is set in Tahoma in the school's own Pages original. Tahoma ships
with Windows and Office and may not be redistributed, so it cannot live in
this repository or on the server that builds the PDFs.

These files are DejaVu Sans Condensed — the conventional free stand-in for
Tahoma and Verdana on Linux, and close to Tahoma in width and colour at the
14pt the letter is set at. Bitstream Vera / DejaVu licence: free to use,
embed and redistribute.

  body.ttf         DejaVuSansCondensed.ttf
  body-bold.ttf    DejaVuSansCondensed-Bold.ttf
  body-italic.ttf  DejaVuSansCondensed-Oblique.ttf
